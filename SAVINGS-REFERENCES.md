# PayPal Savings reference scope

This is a 2025-inspired US PayPal Savings visual demonstration, not a pixel-verified historical reproduction. No dated 2025 Savings recording was supplied. Existing Home and Finances navigation and all five bottom navigation items are preserved.

## Sources checked
- PayPal Savings FAQs: https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/paypal-savings-faqs-help777 — directly checked; documents General Savings, goals, Add Money, frequency, review, and withdrawal to PayPal balance. This is a live document, not an archived 2025 specification.
- Official Savings overview: https://www.paypal.com/us/digital-wallet/manage-money/start-saving — public product context; rates change over time.
- Consumer financial services design portfolio: https://ferentheil.design/paypal-consumer-financial-services — design background, not independent proof of exact 2025 layouts.
- Account review: https://www.businessinsider.com/personal-finance/banking/paypal-savings-account-review — secondary historical context; live reviews change and cannot establish a rate across all of 2025.

## Deliberate limits
The existing 4.00% reference is labeled illustrative, never current or guaranteed. Pending and lifetime interest remain zero: no interest accrues. Goal names and targets are local demo entries, not account applications. Add and withdraw movements use only the existing in-memory sample PayPal balance; no linked funding services are used. Recurring frequencies are review-only and never create schedules. All balances and goals reset on reload. Manage is an informational preview; no tax forms, statements, identity verification, SSNs, account numbers, or personal financial credentials are collected. Samples are not deposits and have no insurance coverage.
